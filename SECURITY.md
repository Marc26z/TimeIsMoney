Do **not** open a public GitHub issue for a security problem.

For sensitive reports, encrypt your message with the PGP key below. The key UID
is `nbub1marc26z <marc26z.twistable058@aleeas.com>`; use that address if your
client requires the key's UID as the recipient. For ordinary correspondence,
use `marc26z@proton.me`.

Please include:

- the affected version;
- a description of the issue and its impact;
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept;
- any suggested fix or mitigation.

I aim to acknowledge reports within 7 days and to provide a resolution timeline
after triage. Please give me a reasonable opportunity to fix the issue before
disclosing it publicly.

## Scope and safe harbor

Test only against your own devices or data. Do not access, modify or delete
another person's data, and do not disrupt services. Good-faith research that
follows this policy will not be treated as hostile.

There is currently no bug bounty.
