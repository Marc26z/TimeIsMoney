How this is calculated

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reports $1,232 as the median usual weekly earnings of full-time wage and salary workers in Q3 2026. Dividing that by a 40-hour week gives $30.80 per hour.

The app divides the price you enter by that hourly figure. The result is rounded to the nearest minute.

  • Earnings are gross pay, before taxes and other deductions. Take-home pay may be lower, so the real cost in hours could be higher.
  • BLS defines full-time as 35 hours or more per week; this app uses a standard 40-hour week for the hourly conversion.
  • The figure covers wage and salary workers nationally. It excludes self-employed workers and is not a personal salary estimate.

Your price never leaves this device, and the app works offline.
